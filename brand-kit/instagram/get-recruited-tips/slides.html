Tip 1
Build a Player Profile
Coaches don't have time to chase you down. They search the Player Directory looking for recruits by position, grad year, and location.

If you don't have a profile, you don't exist to most coaching staffs.

What to include: Position, grad year, GPA, height/weight, current club, and a highlight video link. Make your profile public so every coach on the platform can find you.

Tip 2
Post a Highlight Video
This is the single most important thing you can do. Coaches watch film before they reach out. No video = no interest.

It doesn't have to be professionally edited. Game footage is fine. 2-3 minutes showing your best plays.

Where to host: Upload to YouTube or Hudl, then paste the link in your College Rugby Portal profile. Coaches can watch it directly from your player card.

Tip 3
Favorite Programs You Want
When you favorite a program on the portal, the coaching staff gets notified automatically. Your name, position, and grad year land right in their dashboard.

It's the easiest way to say "I'm interested" without writing a single word.

Pro move: Favorite your top 5-10 programs. Coaches see the interest and often follow up with a message or check out your profile.

Tip 4
Message Coaches Directly
Don't wait for coaches to find you. Reach out first. A short, professional message goes a long way.

Coaches on the portal get email and push notifications when you message them, so they'll see it fast.

What to say: "Hi Coach, I'm [name], a [position] graduating in [year] from [state]. I'm interested in your program because [reason]. Here's my profile: [link]." Keep it short.

Tip 5
Do Your Research
Coaches can tell when a player has actually looked into their program. Use the portal to compare academics, tuition, rankings, and scholarships before reaching out.

Mention something specific about the program in your message. It shows you're serious, not mass-emailing.

Use Compare: Select up to 3 programs to see them side-by-side. It helps you narrow your list and ask better questions when you talk to coaches.
