SEARCH AND FILTER
Browse 900+ programs. Search by name, city, state, or conference, then filter by:
  • Gender — Men's or Women's programs
  • State — Find programs near you or anywhere in the US
  • League — NCR D1, CRAA D1A, NIRA, and more
  • Scholarship — Show only programs offering rugby scholarships
  • School-Funded — Programs funded by the school
  • Min GPA / Max Tuition — Set academic and budget filters
Tip

Click any program to see full details: academics, rankings, tuition, coach contacts, and program notes.

FAVORITES AND COMPARE
F

Save Your Favorites

Tap the heart on any program to save it. Access your list from the Favorites tab anytime.

Important

When you favorite a program, the coaching staff is notified automatically. This is an easy way to get on a coach's radar.

C

Compare Up to 3 Programs

Select programs to compare, then see them side-by-side: academics, tuition, rankings, rugby info, and coach contacts. Best values are highlighted in green.

BUILD YOUR PROFILE
Your profile is how coaches discover you. Complete it to maximize your visibility.

Personal Info

Name, email, phone, high school, city, state, grad year

Academics

GPA, SAT, ACT, intended major

Rugby Background

Position, years playing, club, height, weight, coach info

Highlight Video

Add your Hudl, YouTube, or other video link

Key Step

Toggle "Make my profile visible" to ON. Coaches actively browse the Player Directory to find recruits. A public profile with a highlight video gets the most attention.

Message Coaches Directly
