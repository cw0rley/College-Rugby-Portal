Getting Started

College Rugby Portal helps you discover and compare 900+ college rugby programs across the US. Whether you're a high school player looking for your first program or a club player exploring transfer options, this guide will help you make the most of every feature.

Create Your Account

1
Visit collegerugbyportal.com and tap Sign In in the top right.
2
Sign in with Google or create an account with your email and password.
3
If using email/password, check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to verify.
Tip: Use the email you check most often. Coaches will contact you through the portal's messaging system, and you'll get email notifications when they do.

Install the App (Add to Home Screen)

College Rugby Portal works like a native app on your phone:

  • iPhone: Open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen."
  • Android: Open in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."

The app will launch fullscreen with no browser bar, just like a real app.

Browsing Programs

Search and Filter

The Programs tab is your starting point. Use the search bar to find schools by name, city, state, or conference. Refine results with filters:

  • Gender: Men's or Women's programs
  • State: Filter by location
  • League: NCR D1, CRAA D1A, NIRA, etc.
  • Conference: Narrow to a specific conference
  • Scholarship: Show only programs offering rugby scholarships

Program Detail Pages

Click any program card to see its full detail page, which includes:

Academic Info

Average GPA, SAT scores, acceptance rate, enrollment, US News ranking, and tuition (in-state and out-of-state).

Rugby Info

National ranking, conference, league, scholarship availability, whether the program is school-funded, and program notes.

Coach Contacts

Head coach and staff names, titles, and email addresses. Reach out directly or use the in-app messaging system.

Saving Favorites

Tap the heart icon on any program card or detail page to save it to your favorites. Access all your saved programs from the Favorites tab in the bottom navigation bar.

Important: When you favorite a program, the coaching staff is notified that you're interested. This is a great way to get on a coach's radar without sending a message.

Comparing Programs

1
On any program detail page, tap the Compare button. You can select up to 3 programs.
2
A bar appears at the bottom of the screen showing your selected programs.
3
Tap Compare to see a side-by-side breakdown of academics, tuition, rugby info, rankings, and coach contacts.

The comparison highlights the best values in green (e.g., highest GPA, lowest tuition, best ranking) so you can quickly spot differences.

Your Player Profile

Your profile is how coaches discover you. A complete profile dramatically increases your visibility to coaching staffs across the country.

Creating Your Profile

Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar. Fill in as much as you can:

Personal Info

Name, email, phone, high school, city, state, graduation year.

Academics

GPA, SAT, ACT, intended major. Coaches look at academics first to determine eligibility.

Rugby Background

Primary and secondary positions, years playing, current club/team, height, weight, coach name and email.

Highlight Video

Add a link to your Hudl, YouTube, or other video highlights. This is often the first thing coaches watch.

Achievements and Preferences

Representative selections, rugby achievements, preferred regions, and specific schools you're interested in.

Making Your Profile Public

Toggle "Make my profile visible in the Player Directory" to ON. This allows every coach on the platform to find you by searching for your position, graduation year, location, or name.

Tip: Coaches actively browse the Player Directory looking for recruits. A public profile with a highlight video link gets significantly more attention.

Messaging Coaches

Once you've created a player profile, you can message coaches directly through the portal.

1
Visit a program's detail page and look for the Message button.
2
Write your message and send. Coaches receive email and push notifications.
3
Check the Messages tab in the bottom bar for replies.
Tip: Keep your first message brief and professional. Introduce yourself, mention your position and graduation year, and explain why you're interested in their program.

Notifications

The bell icon in the header shows your notifications. You'll be notified when:

  • A coach sends you a message
  • A coach views your profile or adds you to their recruit list

Enable push notifications when prompted so you don't miss messages even when the app is closed.

Exploring Leagues and Rankings

Leagues

The Leagues page shows every league in US college rugby. Expand any league to see all teams, sorted by national ranking. Each team displays its conference and state.

Rankings

The Rankings page shows nationally ranked programs grouped by league. Toggle between Men's and Women's. Click any program to see its full details.

Conferences

The Conferences page lists all conferences grouped by league. Click a conference to see all the teams in it and their details.

Quick Reference

Bottom Navigation Bar

Programs - Browse all programs | Favorites - Your saved programs | Profile - Your player profile | Messages - Conversations with coaches (appears after profile is created) | More - Conferences, Leagues, Rankings, About

Profile Checklist

For the best results, complete: name, graduation year, GPA, position, height/weight, highlight video link, and make your profile public.
